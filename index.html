02 — Backstory

It used to be a bank.

The Daily Grind sits in the old Santander branch at the back of the Students' Union — the building right next to the nursery. Same bones, new purpose: where students used to queue to pay rent, they now queue for a flat white before the 9am.

We're part of LSU — student-run, student-priced, student-paced. The terrace catches the afternoon sun, the indoor seats fill up around revision week, and the door's open from eight in the morning.

03 — Visit

Find us at the back.

How to find it

Walk through the Students' Union and keep going — the Daily Grind is in the building behind, the one that used to be Santander, sat right next to the nursery. There's a terrace out front; you can usually hear the espresso machine before you see the door.

Cycling in? Stands are at the back of LSU. Driving? The campus is signposted off the A6 — once you're on, follow signs for the Students' Union.
